You have successfully set up your account! Since this is a fresh setup, please follow these steps to build your custom Teacher OS:

  1. Upload Arbor Data: Go to Settings and upload your Master Excel file.
  2. Set Your Calendar: While in Settings, add your Term Dates formatted as DD/MM/YYYY so your Planner aligns to Week A/B.
  3. Configure OS: Add your AI API Key, physical Rooms, and pick your Theme.
  4. Pin Your Classes: Go to the Timetable Builder, search for your specific classes, and click 'Pin'.
  5. Build Timetable: Drag and drop your pinned classes onto the grid.
  6. Ready to Go: You can now build Seating Plans, use the Markbook, and write Lesson Plans!
